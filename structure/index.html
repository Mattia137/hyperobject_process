STRUCTURAL CONCEPT
Hybrid steel–concrete system. Faceted steel space frame forms the primary skin and lateral load path. Three RC cores ensure torsional rigidity and act as vertical stability elements.
STEEL SKIN FRAME
IPE 800 (cyan): perimeter macro-structure spanning large distances. IPE 600 (red/blue): secondary grid for floor and facade loads. SHS 200×200: buckling bracing and torsion resistance.
RC CORES & SLABS
Three C35/45 cores provide shear walls in both principal directions. 500 mm flat slabs span between cores and steel perimeter; max displacement at cantilevered lower-level slabs.
